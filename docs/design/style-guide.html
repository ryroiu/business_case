Type and layout

  • System sans-serif. Body 16 px; card text 14 px; titles 32-40 px; secondary labels 11-13 px.
  • Content width up to 1200 px. Panels use 24-28 px padding, 12 px corners, and a subtle border.
  • Three columns on desktop, two on medium screens, one on phones. Filters stack on narrow screens.
  • Keep service names and owners visible. Omit image space when there is no architecture image.

Controls and language

Tier 1 · Critical

Review overdue - confirm with the owning team.

  • Use descriptive actions: View service, Pin service, Runbook, On-call schedule.
  • Use words with color: never imply service health from a tier badge.
  • Provide visible keyboard focus, labeled filters, and controls at least 44 px high.
  • Design loading, no-results, empty, missing-data, and error states alongside the normal view.
